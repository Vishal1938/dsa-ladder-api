`grid[r][c]` is the cost of a plot of land (all costs are non-negative). Return the side length of the **largest square** of plots whose total cost is at most `budget`, or `0` if even a single plot is too expensive.

**Constraints**
- `1 <= rows, cols <= 200`
- `0 <= grid[r][c] <= 10^4`
- `0 <= budget <= 10^9`
